Our story

From a Kwun Tong desk to six regional hubs.

HK BAOSHUN TRADE LIMITED was founded in 2017 by a small team of senior operators who had spent two decades moving consumer goods between Asia, Europe and North America. We started with one Hong Kong desk, one Shenzhen sourcing office, and a conviction that cross-border trade could be run with the same discipline as a top-tier financial services firm.

Nine years on, we operate six regional hubs, an audited network of 4,800+ factories, and a mobile app that puts every order, shipment and invoice in our clients' pockets. We are still privately held, still based in Hong Kong, and still answering the phone in English within two business hours.

Below is the short version of how we got here.

  1. 2017

    Founded in Kwun Tong, Hong Kong

    Opened our first office at FOOK CHEONG Building, Kwun Tong, with a team of four and a focus on import & export between Mainland China and the EU.

  2. 2019

    First recurring 40HQ programme

    Onboarded our first long-term client on a 40HQ-per-month programme, and added our Shenzhen sourcing office to qualify factories on the ground.

  3. 2021

    Cross-border e-commerce launch

    Launched a dedicated cross-border e-commerce desk covering Amazon FBA, eBay, Etsy and Shopify, and signed our first TikTok Shop creator in Q4.

  4. 2023

    EU hub in Hamburg

    Opened our European hub in Hamburg, with bonded warehouse capacity and an in-house customs broker for the EU and the UK.

  5. 2024

    Mobile app on iOS & Android

    Published the first version of the HK BAOSHUN mobile management app on the App Store and Google Play, giving clients real-time visibility into every order and shipment.

  6. 2026

    New direct rail lane to Duisburg

    Launched a weekly block-train from Xi'an to Duisburg, with on-site customs at the Duisburg terminal, cutting transit time to 14 days for our European clients.

Leadership principles

Five principles, no exceptions.

These are the standards we hold ourselves to, written down so that they cannot drift.

1 · The lane is the unit of truth

We measure ourselves lane by lane, not region by region. Each of our 42 active lanes has an owner, a recovery plan, and a published on-time-in-full number.

2 · Senior operators on every desk

No junior runs a 40HQ programme alone. Every shipment has a named operator with at least seven years of trade experience, and they are reachable in English within two business hours.

3 · Documented, not improvised

If a process is not written down, it is a guess. Every recurring workflow has a runbook, a checklist, and a sample artefact in our quality system.

4 · Mobile-first visibility

If our clients cannot see it on their phone, it does not exist. Every milestone event streams to the HK BAOSHUN mobile app within sixty seconds of the source system.

5 · Compliance is a feature, not a cost

HS codes, dual-use controls, sanctions screening and AEO compliance are baked into every shipment. We do not charge extra for doing the right thing.

A real human, fast

Your account team responds within 2 business hours, in English, from Hong Kong — not from a chatbot, not from a call centre, and never with a "we will get back to you".
