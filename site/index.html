What we do

A full trade stack, one trusted partner.

From a single PO of 100 units to a recurring 40HQ programme, our team and your mobile app cover every step between your supplier and your buyer's door.

Import & Export

End-to-end cross-border movement of general merchandise, consumer goods and industrial supplies, with full documentation.

  • FCL / LCL / Air / Rail China–Europe
  • Customs brokerage in 14 jurisdictions
  • HS-code classification & duties optimisation

Wholesale & Retail

Bulk supply for distributors, chains and independent retailers — with vendor-managed inventory and EDI integration.

  • Case-pack, inner-pack and shelf-ready units
  • Drop-ship and blind-ship fulfilment
  • EDI 850 / 855 / 856 / 810

Cross-Border E-Commerce

List, sell and ship on every major marketplace from one dashboard — with our mobile app as the remote control.

  • Amazon FBA, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, TikTok Shop
  • Shopify Plus & headless storefronts
  • Multi-currency, multi-warehouse, multi-locale

Supply Chain & Logistics

Origin pickup, bonded warehousing in Hong Kong / Shenzhen / Yiwu / Hamburg / LA, and last-mile integration with carriers.

  • FTZ & bonded zone operations
  • Container drayage and yard management
  • Returns & reverse logistics

Consulting & Agency

Market-entry strategy, sourcing audits, brand incubation, IP & trademark filing, and compliance advisory.

  • FDA, CE, UKCA, FCC, RCM, ANVISA
  • Trademark & patent filing in 60+ jurisdictions
  • ESG, modern-slavery & supply-chain due diligence

Mobile Management App

Manage every order, shipment, supplier and invoice from your phone. Available on iOS and Android — Google Play & App Store.

  • Real-time shipment tracking & ETAs
