How we work

A five-step process that scales from sample to programme.

Every engagement runs through the same workflow — documented, audited and visible to you from your phone.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    Send your SKU list, target markets, and timeline. We confirm scope and required documents within one business day.

  2. 02

    Quote

    Line-by-line landed-cost quote in USD: unit price, freight, duty, tax, last-mile. Valid for 14 days.

  3. 03

    Compliance

    HS classification, certifications, labelling, dual-use and sanctions screening. We file what needs filing.

  4. 04

    Fulfilment

    Production, QC, origin pickup, freight, customs, warehousing, last-mile. Every milestone lands in your app.

  5. 05

    Settlement

    Invoice, payment, FX, statements. We reconcile every PO and surface a monthly margin report per SKU.
